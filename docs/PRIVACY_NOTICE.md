# easyACR Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2026-09-02

easyACR uses a signed browser session to authorize a scan. Depending on deployment configuration, it may also use an external sign-in provider. It processes the submitted URL, scan status, and structured automated findings to run and display the requested scan. It does not accept submitted credentials or intentionally retain raw page HTML.

Hosted durable scan records and findings are configured for deletion after 30 days; local invite-mode work may be cleared when the service restarts. Do not submit sensitive, personal, confidential, or regulated information through a scan target. Contact the service operator for deployment-specific retention and deletion information. This is beta-launch copy and requires operator/legal review before a commercial release.

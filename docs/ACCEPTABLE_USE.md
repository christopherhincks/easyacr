# easyACR Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-02

Do not scan a target without permission; evade controls; use easyACR for denial-of-service, security testing, data collection, or credential harvesting; or make unsupported accessibility or legal claims from automated findings. We may suspend access, cancel jobs, and retain necessary security records for suspected misuse.

This is beta-launch copy and requires operator/legal review before commercial release.
